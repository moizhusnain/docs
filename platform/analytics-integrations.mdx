The AI CMO runs a full SEO audit when you set up your project, but that audit is a snapshot. To understand what is actually happening on your site day to day, you need live data: who is visiting, where they are coming from, which pages they land on, what they search for, and how your rankings are moving. That is what Google Analytics and Google Search Console give you.

Both are free. Connecting them takes a few minutes and you only do it once. Once connected, both data sources feed into a single consolidated analytics view inside your CMO project.

### **Why Connect Them**

Without these connections, the AI CMO works from the SEO audit alone. It can tell you what is technically broken or under-optimised, but it cannot tell you whether traffic is growing, which content is working, or what your audience is actually searching for.

Once you connect Google Analytics, the CMO knows your real traffic numbers: sessions, users, engagement, what sources are sending visitors, and how that compares to the previous period.

Once you connect Search Console, it knows your keyword footprint: which queries people use to find you, how many clicks and impressions each generates, and where your average ranking position sits.

With both live, the CMO presents everything in one unified view and can give you answers grounded in real evidence rather than static audit data.

### **Connecting Google Analytics**

**Step 1: Open the connection prompt**

Go to your CMO project. In the Analytics panel on the right side of the dashboard, click the link icon in the header. Alternatively, go to Settings and open the Integrations tab. Under Analytics, you will see the Google Analytics option.

**Step 2: Authorise via Google**

Click **Google Analytics**. You will be taken to Google to sign in and authorise the connection. Once done, you are brought back to Okara automatically.

**Step 3: Select a property**

After connecting, you will be asked to choose which Google Analytics 4 property to track. If your Google account manages multiple properties, they will all appear in a dropdown. Select the one for this site and click **Connect Property**.

### **Connecting Google Search Console**

**Step 1: Open the connection prompt**

Same path as Google Analytics: the link icon in the Analytics panel header, or Settings → Integrations → Analytics.

**Step 2: Authorise via Google**

Click **Google Search Console**. You will be taken to Google for OAuth authorisation and then returned automatically.

**Step 3: Select a site**

Your verified Search Console properties will appear in a dropdown. Select the one matching this CMO project. If no verified sites appear, you need to verify ownership in Google Search Console first before the connection will work. Click **Connect Property** once you have made a selection.

### **What the Analytics Tab Shows You**

Once both services are connected, your data appears in a single consolidated tab. Everything is in one place — search visibility and site behaviour side by side.

**How people found you**

A top-level funnel shows the full journey from search impression to site visit: how many people saw you in Google, how many clicked through, and how many visited your site. The click rate and a comparison to traffic from other channels are shown alongside, so you can see how your organic search performance stacks up against direct and social traffic at a glance.

**Traffic over time**

A line chart plots visits and search clicks over the last 7 days on the same axis. This makes it easy to spot whether a drop in visits is search-driven or coming from elsewhere.

### **Asking the CMO About Your Data**

Once both connections are live, you can ask the AI CMO questions in chat and it will read directly from your analytics data to answer.

<Callout icon="pencil">
  Which search queries should I be writing more content about?
</Callout>

<Callout icon="pencil">
  My organic traffic dropped this month. What does the data show?
</Callout>

<Callout icon="pencil">
  What are my most visited pages and how is their engagement?
</Callout>

<Callout icon="pencil">
  Which channel is sending me the most engaged users?
</Callout>

The CMO pulls from both the Google Analytics and Search Console results stored for your project. If a connection is missing or data has not synced yet, it will tell you clearly and explain what to connect. If the data is there, every answer it gives you is grounded in the actual numbers for your site.
