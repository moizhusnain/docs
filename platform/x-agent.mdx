Building a following on X does not have to mean sitting down every morning to figure out what to say. The CMO writes a daily post for you, grounded in your product positioning and what is resonating in your niche. You review it, post it, and build a compounding presence without it eating your week.

For LinkedIn post generation, see the [LinkedIn Agent](/platform/linkedin-agent) page.

### **What the CMO Writes Every Day**

Every day the CMO analyses your product, your competitors, and current conversations in your industry. From that it drafts one X post, ready to publish. The post is written to reflect your positioning, not generic advice. It may be a thought on a problem your customers face, a contrarian take in your space, or a direct observation about your market.

The post lands in your Feed alongside your other daily tasks.

### **Reviewing a Post**

Click any X post card in your Feed to open the full view. You will see the complete draft, the reasoning the CMO used to write it, and your action options.

Read it before you post. The CMO writes with your product in mind, but you know your audience better. If the angle is off, the tone is wrong, or you want to take it in a different direction, you have two options.

**Edit it yourself**: click the edit icon inside the post sheet to make changes directly.

**Ask the CMO to rewrite it**: click **Rewrite with CMO**. This opens the chat with that specific post pre-loaded. Tell the CMO what you want changed.

<Callout icon="pencil">
  Make this shorter. Cut the second sentence and lead with the question.
</Callout>

<Callout icon="pencil">
  Rewrite for a more technical audience who already knows what SEO is.
</Callout>

The CMO will return a revised version you can post immediately. You can also go to the chat at any time and ask for an entirely new post on a topic you have in mind.

### **Posting to X**

You do not need to connect anything for X. Once you are happy with the post, click **Post** in the top right of the card. This opens X with the tweet text pre-filled. Review it and hit post.

If you want a different version before sending, use the **Rewrite with CMO** button to open the chat and iterate first.

### **After Posting**

Once a post goes live, Okara marks it as complete in your Feed and stores a link to the published post. If you need to find it later, it will be in your archived tasks.

### **Tuning Your Voice with Writing Instructions**

The CMO applies a default writing style for X, but you can override it with your own instructions. This is how you make every post sound like you wrote it.

Open the Writing Instructions panel from the settings icon on your Feed and find the X field.

Good X instructions might specify:

- Short, punchy lines with no preamble
- Contrarian or question-based hooks
- Whether to include a CTA and how direct it should be
- Things to avoid: corporate language, vague statements, hashtag overload

Type your instructions and click **Save**. The CMO will apply them to every X post it writes from then on.
