Building a following on X and LinkedIn does not have to mean sitting down every morning to figure out what to say. The AI CMO writes and queues social content for you daily, grounded in your product positioning and what is resonating in your niche. You review it, post it, and build a compounding presence without it eating your week.

This guide covers how the X and LinkedIn agents work, how to connect your accounts for one-click posting, and how to tune the content to match your voice.

### **What the CMO Writes Every Day**

Every day the AI CMO analyses your product, your competitors, and current conversations in your industry. From that it drafts one X post ready to publish. The post is written to reflect your positioning, not generic advice. It may be a thought on a problem your customers face, a contrarian take in your space, or a direct observation about your market.

LinkedIn post generation is available on the Max plan and follows the same logic: one post per day, written with the professional tone LinkedIn audiences respond to.

Both land in your Feed alongside your other daily tasks.

### **Reviewing a Post**

Click any post card in your Feed to open the full view. You will see the complete draft, the reasoning the CMO used to write it, and your action options.

Read it before you post. The CMO writes with your product in mind, but you know your audience better. If the angle is off, the tone is too formal, or you want to take it in a different direction, you have two options.

**Edit it yourself**: click the edit icon inside the post sheet to make changes directly.

**Ask the CMO to rewrite it**: click **Rewrite with CMO**. This opens the chat with that specific post pre-loaded. Tell the CMO what you want changed.

<Callout icon="pencil">
  Make this shorter. Cut the second sentence and lead with the question.
</Callout>

<Callout icon="pencil">
  Rewrite for a more technical audience who already knows what SEO is.
</Callout>

The CMO will return a revised version you can post immediately. You can also go to the chat at any time and ask for an entirely new post on a topic you have in mind.

### **Posting to X**

For X, you do not need to connect anything. Once you are happy with the post, click **Post** in the top right of the card. This opens X with the tweet text pre-filled. Review it and hit post.

If you want a different version of the post before sending, use the **Rewrite with CMO** button to open the chat and iterate first.

### **Connecting LinkedIn for Direct Posting**

LinkedIn is where direct posting makes the biggest difference. Instead of copying text and switching tabs, you connect your LinkedIn account once and post from inside Okara in a single click.

**To connect LinkedIn:**

1. Open the post sheet for any LinkedIn post in your Feed
2. Click **Post to LinkedIn**
3. You will be taken to LinkedIn to authorise the connection
4. Once authorised, you are brought back to Okara automatically

From that point on, clicking **Post to LinkedIn** publishes the post directly without leaving the dashboard.

You can also connect from the Integrations tab. Go to your CMO project settings and open **Integrations**. Under **Socials**, you will see LinkedIn listed with a **Connect** button. The same OAuth flow applies.

### **After Posting**

Once a post goes live, Okara marks it as complete in your Feed and stores a link to the published post. If you need to find it later, it will be in your archived tasks.

### **Tuning the Voice with Writing Instructions**

The CMO applies a default writing style for each platform, but you can override it with your own instructions. This is how you make every post sound like you wrote it.

Open the Writing Instructions panel from the settings icon on your Feed. Inside, you will find separate instruction fields for X and LinkedIn.

For X, good instructions might specify:

- Short, punchy lines with no preamble
- Contrarian or question-based hooks
- Whether to include a CTA and how direct it should be
- Things to avoid: corporate language, vague statements, hashtag overload

For LinkedIn, you might specify:

- Founder voice, first person, story-led
- Short paragraphs with white space
- A clear practical takeaway at the end
- Tone: direct but warm, not formal

Type your instructions and click **Save**. The CMO will apply them to every post it writes for that platform from then on.
