Short-form video is running the internet right now. Reels, TikToks, Shorts; whatever the platform calls them, the format is the same. A strong hook in the first two seconds, a clear message, and something that feels real. This is what gets watched, shared, and remembered. And it is increasingly how people discover new products and brands for the first time.

The problem is that making this kind of content takes effort. You need a script that lands. You need visuals that hold attention. You need to produce something that feels polished but not corporate. For most teams, especially lean ones, video creation ends up on the "we should really do that" list and stays there.

Okara's UGC Video Creator changes that. It uses advanced AI video and audio generation to help you go from a rough idea to a finished, download-ready video without leaving the platform. No filming. No editing software. No back-and-forth with freelancers. You describe what you want, and the CMO builds it.

### Start with an Idea

Open your Feed and click on the **UGC Video** tab. This is where all your video projects live.

To create a new video, open the chat and share your idea. It does not need to be polished. A sentence or two about what you want to communicate is enough. Something like "a quick video showing why our onboarding is faster than competitors" or "a reel highlighting three features people do not know about" works perfectly.

The CMO will take your idea and turn it into a full script: structured with a hook, a body, and a close. It writes for the format, so the pacing and tone are already built for short attention spans.

### Review and Edit Your Script

Once the script is ready, a panel opens with the full breakdown. Read through it carefully. This is your video, so make sure it sounds like you.

You can edit the script directly in the panel. Change the wording, adjust the hook, move sections around, cut anything that feels unnecessary. 

Take your time here. The quality of the script is what makes the difference between a video people scroll past and one they actually watch.

### Choose Your Format

Below the script, you will find the format settings. There are two things to configure: aspect ratio and resolution.

**Aspect ratio** depends on where the video is going. Pick the one that matches your platform:

- **9:16** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts. Vertical, full screen on mobile. This is the default for most short-form content.
- **16:9** YouTube. Standard widescreen, built for landscape viewing.
- **1:1** Instagram feed posts. Square format that works well in the grid.
- **4:3** Classic format. Works for presentations, product demos, or anywhere a wider frame helps.
- **3:4** Portrait. A taller frame that gives more vertical space than 1:1 but is not as narrow as 9:16.
- **21:9** Cinematic. Ultra-wide format for a more dramatic, film-like feel.

**Resolution** can be set between 480p and 720p. Higher resolution gives you a crisper image but takes longer to generate. For most social content, 720p is the right choice.

### Generate and Download

Once your script is locked in and your format is set, click **Generate**.

This part takes some time. The AI is building your video frame by frame, generating voice, syncing audio, and assembling everything into a finished file. You do not need to stay on the page. Come back when it is done.

Once the video is ready, you can preview it directly in Okara. Watch it through. If you are happy with it, click **Download** and the file is yours. Upload it to TikTok, drop it into your Instagram queue, publish it on YouTube. Wherever it needs to go.
