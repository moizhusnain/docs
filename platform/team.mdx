Most growth tools are built for one person with one website. The AI CMO is designed to scale with you. You can bring your team in to collaborate on the same project, and run separate CMO projects for every site in your portfolio. This guide covers both.

### **Inviting Team Members**

Every CMO project can have multiple members. The project owner is the admin and each invited person gets member access. Members can use the full CMO: chat, review the Feed, write and post content, and access all the analytics. The billing stays with the project owner.

**To invite a team member:**

1. Open your CMO project
2. Click the **Avatar** in the top right corner of the CMO dashboard.
3. Click on **Add Team Member** option, this will take you to the [https://okara.ai/settings?tab=team](https://okara.ai/settings?tab=team)
4. To add a new member, you would first need to add a new seat. Select the number of seats you want to add in your team, and it will take you to the Stripe for payment.
5. Enter the email address of the person you want to invite
6. Click **Send** **Invite**

The invitation is sent by email. Once they accept, they will appear in the members list with their role and the date they joined.

**Seats**

Each CMO project comes with a set number of seats included in your plan. The first seat requires you to complete a check out flow. Any subsequent seats will be added automatically to your plan.

This is a very important consideration. Adding new members will not require you to go through the checkout again and your card will be automatically charged for the new seat. Deleting the user invite will not remove the charge from your account and the seat will be added to your quota. You can always manage your seats through [https://okara.ai/settings?tab=subscription](https://okara.ai/settings?tab=subscription) \> Manage Subscriptions.

To remove someone's access, click the remove icon next to their name in the members list.

### **Managing Multiple Projects**

If you run more than one website or brand, each one can have its own CMO project. Each project has its own Feed, analytics, product documents, writing instructions, and chat history. They run completely independently.

**To add a new project:**

1. Click the project name in the top left of your CMO dashboard
2. A dropdown will show all your current projects
3. Click **Add Project** at the bottom of the list
4. You will go through a checkout flow to activate the new project

Once activated, the new project goes through the same onboarding as your first one: website scan, strategy documents, SEO audit, and content setup. After that it starts generating its own daily tasks in its own Feed.

You can switch between projects at any time from the project switcher in the top left.

The AI CMO is built to support a real marketing operation. Whether that means one person running several brands or a full team working on a single project, the same tools, Feed, and chat are available to everyone involved.
