Every CMO project can have multiple members. Bring your team in to collaborate on the same project — everyone gets access to the full CMO: chat, the Feed, content review, and analytics. The billing stays with the project owner.

For running separate CMO projects across multiple websites or brands, see [Multiple Websites](/platform/websites).

### **Inviting Team Members**

**To invite a team member:**

1. Open your CMO project
2. Click the **Avatar** in the top right corner of the CMO dashboard
3. Click **Add Team Member** — this takes you to your [team settings](https://okara.ai/settings?tab=team)
4. To add a new member, first add a new seat. Select the number of seats you want and you will be taken to Stripe for payment
5. Enter the email address of the person you want to invite
6. Click **Send Invite**

The invitation is sent by email. Once they accept, they will appear in the members list with their role and the date they joined.

### **Seats**

Each CMO project comes with a set number of seats included in your plan. The first seat requires you to complete a checkout flow. Any subsequent seats are added automatically to your plan.

A few things worth knowing before you add members:

- Adding a new member triggers an automatic charge for the new seat — you will not go through checkout again.
- Deleting a user invite does not remove the charge. The seat is added to your quota regardless.
- You can manage your seats at any time through [Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscriptions](https://okara.ai/settings?tab=subscription).

To remove someone's access, click the remove icon next to their name in the members list.
