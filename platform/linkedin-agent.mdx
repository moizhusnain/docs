LinkedIn rewards consistency more than any other platform. One post a day, written in a founder voice with a clear takeaway, compounds into real visibility over months. The problem is most teams do not have the time to write that post every day. The CMO does it for you.

LinkedIn post generation is available on the Max plan. For X post generation, see the [X Agent](/platform/x-agent) page.

### **What the CMO Writes Every Day**

Every day the CMO analyses your product, your competitors, and current conversations in your industry. From that it drafts one LinkedIn post, ready to review. The post is written with the professional tone LinkedIn audiences respond to — story-led, first person, with a clear practical takeaway.

It lands in your Feed alongside your other daily tasks.

### **Reviewing a Post**

Click any LinkedIn post card in your Feed to open the full view. You will see the complete draft, the reasoning the CMO used to write it, and your action options.

Read it before you post. The CMO writes with your product in mind, but you know your audience better. If the angle is off, the tone is too formal, or you want to take it in a different direction, you have two options.

**Edit it yourself**: click the edit icon inside the post sheet to make changes directly.

**Ask the CMO to rewrite it**: click **Rewrite with CMO**. This opens the chat with that specific post pre-loaded. Tell the CMO what you want changed.

<Callout icon="pencil">
  Make this more story-led. Start with the problem I faced, not the solution.
</Callout>

<Callout icon="pencil">
  Rewrite for a B2B SaaS audience. Less founder-speak, more business outcome.
</Callout>

The CMO will return a revised version you can post immediately. You can also go to the chat at any time and ask for a post on a specific topic.

### **Connecting LinkedIn for Direct Posting**

Direct posting makes the biggest difference on LinkedIn. Instead of copying text and switching tabs, you connect your account once and post from inside Okara in a single click.

**To connect LinkedIn:**

1. Open the post sheet for any LinkedIn post in your Feed
2. Click **Post to LinkedIn**
3. You will be taken to LinkedIn to authorise the connection
4. Once authorised, you are brought back to Okara automatically

From that point on, clicking **Post to LinkedIn** publishes the post directly without leaving the dashboard.

You can also connect from the Integrations tab. Go to your CMO project settings and open **Integrations**. Under **Socials**, you will see LinkedIn listed with a **Connect** button. The same OAuth flow applies.

### **After Posting**

Once a post goes live, Okara marks it as complete in your Feed and stores a link to the published post. If you need to find it later, it will be in your archived tasks.

### **Tuning Your Voice with Writing Instructions**

The CMO applies a default writing style for LinkedIn, but you can override it with your own instructions. This is how you make every post sound like you wrote it.

Open the Writing Instructions panel from the settings icon on your Feed and find the LinkedIn field.

Good LinkedIn instructions might specify:

- Founder voice, first person, story-led
- Short paragraphs with white space between them
- A clear practical takeaway at the end
- Tone: direct but warm, not formal
- Things to avoid: buzzwords, passive voice, generic motivational content

Type your instructions and click **Save**. The CMO will apply them to every LinkedIn post it writes from then on.
