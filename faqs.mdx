#### **How can I change my website at Okara AI CMO?**

You can change your website URL through settings

1. Navigate to **Settings** through profile dropdown (Profile icon on the top left of CMO screen)
2. Click the **Websites** tab from the sidebar
3. Under **Change Website URL**, enter your new website URL
4. Click to → the change

**Important:** Changing your product URL will permanently delete all existing data for your current website, including your SEO reports, analytics, Reddit mentions, generated articles and content, competitor analysis and strategy documents.

The system will then restart the full onboarding analysis for your new website. This action cannot be undone, so make sure you want to switch before confirming.

<Info>
  You cannot change the URL while onboarding is in progress. Wait for the current analysis to complete first.
</Info>

#### **My Dashboard is not working?**

If your dashboard isn't loading or showing data or giving an error, try these steps:

1. **Refresh your browser**: Sometimes cached data needs to be cleared
2. **Check your onboarding status**: If the agent is still in "onboarding" status, the dashboard will show limited data until the setup completes (typically 2-5 minutes for free users, 3-6 minutes for Max users)
3. **Verify your connection**: Ensure you have a stable internet connection
4. **Clear browser cache**: Hard refresh with `Cmd+Shift+R` (Mac) or `Ctrl+Shift+R` (Windows)

If onboarding fails:

- A **refresh/retry button** will appear on your dashboard within 1 hour
- Click the button to restart the onboarding process
- The system will retry from the failed phase without losing previous progress

If the issue persists or the retry button doesn't appear, please email [**support@okara.ai**](mailto:support@okara.ai) with your agent ID and a screenshot of any error messages.

#### **I have changed my CMO documents, how can I refresh opportunities?**

Opportunities are automatically refreshed every day.

When you update your marketing strategy documents, they will be used in the next scheduled update to generate fresh opportunities. You don't need to manually trigger a refresh.

<Info>
  Existing opportunities in your feed won't change, but new opportunities discovered after the next 6-hour cycle will reflect your updated strategy.
</Info>

#### **The SEO and GEO changes are not reflecting in audit, instantly?**

SEO and GEO audits are automatically refreshed every day. The system re-analyzes your website and checks current performance.

Why changes don't appear instantly:

- Your website is re-analyzed for technical SEO, meta tags, and content structure
- Performance metrics like page speed are recalculated
- Backlink data is updated (Max users only)
- Changes you make to your website won't appear in search results until Google re-indexes your site (this can take days or weeks)

The audit shows what search engines currently see. Even if you just updated your website, those changes won't appear in our audit until search engines have crawled and indexed them.

You can find this option from the profile dropdown as well. Where it is listed as **Add Website**.

Adding a new websites requires you to upgrades immediately and create a new CMO instance. 

#### **How can I change my email address?**

You can change your email address through **Settings \> Account & Security**

1. Navigate to **Settings** (Profile icon on top right)
2. Click the **Account & Security** tab
3. Click **Change Email** button
4. Choose your method:
   - **Email verification** - Enter your new email and verify through a confirmation link
   - **Google account** - Sign in with a Google account to update your email

After verification, your email will be updated and you'll use the new email for login.

<Info>
  The new email address must not already be associated with another Okara account.
</Info>

#### **How long does the initial onboarding take?**

Onboarding time varies by plan. For free users, it takes about 3-5 minutes. Durning this time, CMO does a complete audit of your website, find competitors and create the strategy documents.

Max onboarding takes another 5-8 minutes. First we run a more detailed re-audit of your website. We spend extra time on creating the strategy and use the strategy document to Create first set of opportunities for you, ranging from Reddit, to Article generation and more.

You can track progress in real-time via the onboarding terminal. The system automatically saves progress, so if something fails, it continues from where it left off.

#### **What happens if onboarding fails?**

Our system has built-in resilience:

1. **Automatic retries**: The system tries up to 3 times if something fails
2. **Progress saved**: Previous work is never lost
3. **Error notifications**: You'll see "This is taking longer than expected" if there's an issue
4. **Team alerts**: Our support team is automatically notified

If onboarding fails:

- Check your website is accessible (not password-protected or blocked)
- Verify your URL is correct
- Use the retry button that appears on your dashboard
- Contact [**support@okara.ai**](mailto:support@okara.ai) with your agent ID if issues persist

Our team can help resolve issues without re-running the entire analysis.

#### **How accurate is the competitor analysis?**

**T**he competitor analysis uses multiple data sources:

**Data sources:**

- Search engine analysis identifies who ranks for your target keywords
- Website analysis examines competitor positioning and messaging
- AI-powered research discovers competitors you might not know about
- Reddit discussions reveal competitors mentioned in your industry

**Accuracy factors:**

- **Best for obvious competitors**: High accuracy for direct competitors
- **May miss niche players**: Smaller competitors might not appear
- **Updated during onboarding**: Reflects state at time of analysis
- **Evolves with your input**: You can add competitors via chat

**To improve accuracy:**

- Provide clear product description during onboarding
- Use specific industry keywords
- Mention known competitors in chat conversations
- Refresh documents periodically as market evolves

#### **What types of articles does AI CMO generate?**

For Max/Founding users, AI CMO generates article topics based on:

**Topic sources:**

- **SEO keyword gaps**: Opportunities where competitors rank but you don't
- **Reddit questions**: Common questions from your target audience
- **Trending discussions**: Emerging topics in your space

**Article metadata includes:**

- **Target keyword**: Primary SEO keyword

- **Estimated word count**: Suggested article length

- **Recommended approach**: Positioning and angle

**Generation process:**

- Topics appear as opportunities in your feed
- You approve topics you want to write
- AI can generate full draft articles
- Articles optimized for both readers and search engines
